30 Journal Prompts for Beginners

simple questions for a first blank page

Here are all 30 prompts, free to read and use.

Take a few minutes with them, or save this page and come back when you need a place to begin. There is no right order and nothing you need to finish.

Start Simple

  1. How am I, actually?
  2. What is on my mind right now?
  3. What happened today that I noticed?
  4. What do I want to remember about today?
  5. What is bothering me, even slightly?
  6. What went fine that I did not mention?
  7. What am I looking forward to?
  8. What would make tomorrow easier?
  9. What do I keep meaning to write down?
  10. What is one true sentence about today?

A Daily Habit

  1. What is one thing I am carrying today?
  2. What can wait until tomorrow?
  3. What did I decide today?
  4. Who did I talk to that mattered?
  5. What drained me?
  6. What gave something back?
  7. What did I say yes to?
  8. What did I say no to?
  9. What do I want more of tomorrow?
  10. How do I want today to end?

When The Page Is Blank

  1. Why am I finding this hard to start?
  2. What would I write if no one could read it?
  3. What am I avoiding putting into words?
  4. What is the most boring true thing today?
  5. What would I tell a friend about this week?
  6. What have I been thinking about too long?
  7. What question do I keep circling?
  8. What am I pretending not to feel?
  9. What would three sentences be enough for?
  10. What can I stop trying to say perfectly?
