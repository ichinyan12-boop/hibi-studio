30 Journal Prompts for Mindfulness

for coming back to the room you are in

All 30 questions are here, free to read and use. If you would rather write them on paper, the printable version is below.

Right Now

  1. What can I hear that I was ignoring?
  2. What is the temperature of this room?
  3. Where are my shoulders?
  4. What is my breath doing?
  5. What colour is nearest to me?
  6. What am I touching?
  7. What does this moment not require?
  8. What am I rushing towards?
  9. What is already finished?
  10. What can wait ten minutes?

The Body Knows First

  1. Where do I hold today?
  2. What does tired feel like, exactly?
  3. When did I last drink water?
  4. What does my jaw do when I read messages?
  5. Which part of me wants to move?
  6. What would help in the next five minutes?
  7. When did I last stand outside?
  8. What is my body asking for, plainly?
  9. What am I ignoring because it is small?
  10. What would rest actually look like?

Slowing Down

  1. What am I doing at double speed?
  2. What would half speed cost me?
  3. Which task am I doing to avoid another?
  4. What am I checking too often?
  5. What would I notice if I put the phone down?
  6. What is the next single thing?
  7. What can be done badly and still count?
  8. What am I finishing only out of habit?
  9. Where can I leave a gap tomorrow?
  10. What deserves my full attention today?
