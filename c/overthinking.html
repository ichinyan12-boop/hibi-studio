30 Journal Prompts for a Mind That Will Not Switch Off

for a mind that will not switch off

All 30 questions are here, free to read and use. If you would rather write them on paper, the printable version is below.

When Thoughts Race

  1. What is loudest right now?
  2. Write it all down. Nothing is too small.
  3. Which one belongs to someone else?
  4. What only needs a place, not a decision?
  5. What am I afraid of if I stop?
  6. What did I decide today, but keep re-deciding?
  7. What is unfinished, and does it matter?
  8. What would I tell a friend saying this?
  9. What can wait until morning?
  10. What will I leave open tomorrow?

When Every Choice Feels Heavy

  1. What am I deciding over and over?
  2. What did I already decide?
  3. Why do I keep reopening it?
  4. What would make it final?
  5. Which choices do not matter?
  6. Which one actually does?
  7. What can I decide once, for a month?
  8. Who else could decide this?
  9. What happens if I do nothing?
  10. What is the smallest next step?

When It Is Time To Let Go

  1. What am I still holding?
  2. When did I pick it up?
  3. What was it protecting me from?
  4. Is that still true?
  5. What would I lose by putting it down?
  6. What would I gain?
  7. Who would notice?
  8. What is the smallest piece to release?
  9. What will I keep on purpose?
  10. How will I know it is done?
