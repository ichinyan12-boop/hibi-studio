30 Journal Prompts for Letting Go

for control, guilt and the fear underneath

All 30 questions are here, free to read and use. If you would rather write them on paper, the printable version is below.

Control

  1. What am I trying to hold in place?
  2. What would happen if I stopped?
  3. Which outcome am I rehearsing?
  4. What is genuinely mine to decide?
  5. What am I managing that is not my job?
  6. Who am I protecting by staying in charge?
  7. What does holding on cost me each week?
  8. What would loosening my grip look like?
  9. What could I hand over today?
  10. What stays fine even if I do nothing?

Guilt

  1. What do I still apologize for silently?
  2. Was it actually mine to carry?
  3. What would I say to a friend in my place?
  4. What did I not know at the time?
  5. What have I already paid for enough?
  6. Who benefits from me feeling this?
  7. What would making it right look like?
  8. Is that possible, or is it just punishment?
  9. What would I forgive if it were someone else?
  10. What am I ready to set down?

Fear

  1. What am I afraid will happen if I move on?
  2. How likely is that, honestly?
  3. What would I do if it happened?
  4. What is the fear protecting me from?
  5. When did it last actually come true?
  6. What am I avoiding by staying stuck?
  7. What does the fear want me to keep?
  8. What is the smallest safe step forward?
  9. Who could I tell about this?
  10. What will I feel once it is behind me?
