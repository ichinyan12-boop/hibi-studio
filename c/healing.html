30 Journal Prompts for Healing

slow questions for the long way back

All 30 questions are here, free to read and use. If you would rather write them on paper, the printable version is below.

What Still Aches

  1. What still catches me off guard?
  2. Which anniversary does my body remember?
  3. What do I wish someone had said?
  4. What did I have to explain too many times?
  5. Which sentence still stings?
  6. What did I lose that no one noticed?
  7. What am I still waiting for an apology for?
  8. What would closure actually look like?
  9. What am I tired of carrying?
  10. What do I need to say out loud once?

What Helped

  1. What got me through the worst week?
  2. Who stayed without being asked?
  3. Which small routine held me together?
  4. What did I do that I am proud of?
  5. When did it first feel slightly lighter?
  6. What place felt safe?
  7. What did I stop doing that helped?
  8. Which kindness do I still remember?
  9. What did I learn to ask for?
  10. What helped that I would recommend?

What I Am Building

  1. What do I want more of next year?
  2. What am I no longer available for?
  3. Which boundary is holding well?
  4. What does a good ordinary day look like?
  5. Who do I want to become slowly?
  6. What am I allowed to want?
  7. What would I like to be easy?
  8. What am I practising, badly, on purpose?
  9. What will I not explain again?
  10. What is the next small honest step?
