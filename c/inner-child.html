30 Journal Prompts for the Inner Child

for the child who is still waiting to be heard

All 30 questions are here, free to read and use. If you would rather write them on paper, the printable version is below.

What You Needed

  1. What did I need to hear at eight years old?
  2. Who did I want to notice me?
  3. What was I never asked about?
  4. Where did I feel safest as a child?
  5. What made me laugh without trying?
  6. What did I love before it was useful?
  7. What did I stop doing to keep the peace?
  8. Which comfort did I have to give up early?
  9. What did I want that felt too big to ask?
  10. Who made me feel ordinary, in a good way?

What You Learned Instead

  1. What did I learn to do to stay liked?
  2. Which feeling was inconvenient at home?
  3. What happened when I was too much?
  4. What happened when I was too quiet?
  5. Which mistake still makes me flinch?
  6. What did being good get me?
  7. Whose mood did I learn to read first?
  8. What did I decide about myself back then?
  9. Is that decision still running things?
  10. Who would I be if I had not learned it?

What I Can Give Now

  1. What would I say to that child today?
  2. What does that part of me still ask for?
  3. What small thing can I give it this week?
  4. Where can I be gentler than I was taught?
  5. What am I allowed to enjoy for no reason?
  6. What rest did I never get permission for?
  7. Who is safe with the younger part of me?
  8. What would a good day have looked like then?
  9. What would it look like now?
  10. What do I want to stop repeating?
