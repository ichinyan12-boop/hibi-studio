30 Self Worth Journal Prompts

for the beliefs you never chose out loud

All 30 questions are here, free to read and use. If you would rather write them on paper, the printable version is below.

What I Believe

  1. What do I believe I have to earn?
  2. What makes me feel like a burden?
  3. When do I feel I have to be useful?
  4. What compliment do I deflect every time?
  5. What do I say about myself as a joke?
  6. Would I say that to someone I love?
  7. What do I think I am not allowed to want?
  8. Where do I make myself smaller?
  9. Who am I trying not to inconvenience?
  10. What do I think happens if I rest?

Where It Came From

  1. Who first made me doubt this?
  2. What was I praised for as a child?
  3. What was ignored?
  4. Which comparison do I still carry?
  5. Whose approval did I chase longest?
  6. What did I have to do to be enough?
  7. Which failure do I still use against myself?
  8. Who told me that was who I am?
  9. Were they right, or just loud?
  10. What did I decide because of them?

What Is True Now

  1. What have I done that was hard?
  2. What do people rely on me for?
  3. What did I get through alone?
  4. What have I learned that I once could not?
  5. Which kindness of mine went unnoticed?
  6. What would my closest friend say about me?
  7. What am I proud of that no one saw?
  8. What is true about me on a bad day?
  9. What am I no longer willing to accept?
  10. What is one honest, kind sentence about me?
