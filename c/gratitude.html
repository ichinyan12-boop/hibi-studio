30 Gratitude Journal Prompts

for noticing what is already here

All 30 questions are here, free to read and use. If you would rather write them on paper, the printable version is below.

Ordinary Things

  1. What made today easier than it had to be?
  2. What did my body do for me today?
  3. Which small comfort did I almost miss?
  4. What sound do I like in this room?
  5. What is working, quietly, right now?
  6. What did I not have to worry about today?
  7. Which object do I use every single day?
  8. What food did I actually taste?
  9. Where did I feel warm today?
  10. What am I glad is over?

People

  1. Who made something lighter for me lately?
  2. Who has never asked me to be different?
  3. Whose message did I reread?
  4. Who taught me something without meaning to?
  5. Who would I call at two in the morning?
  6. What did someone do that I never thanked them for?
  7. Who is easy to be quiet with?
  8. Who believed me when I was unsure?
  9. Which stranger was kind this month?
  10. Who am I glad I stopped knowing?

Hard Things

  1. What did a difficult year give me?
  2. What am I grateful I said no to?
  3. Which mistake taught me the most?
  4. What did waiting make possible?
  5. What am I glad did not work out?
  6. Which fear turned out to be smaller?
  7. What did I survive that I rarely mention?
  8. What did I learn to do alone?
  9. What am I no longer afraid of?
  10. What would I thank my past self for?
