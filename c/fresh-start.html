30 Journal Prompts for a Fresh Start

for beginning again, without the pressure

Here are all 30 prompts, free to read and use.

Take a few minutes with them, or save this page and come back when you need a place to begin. There is no right order and nothing you need to finish.

A New Month

  1. What filled last month?
  2. What am I glad I did?
  3. What kept getting pushed?
  4. What did I outgrow?
  5. What kept returning?
  6. What do I want less of?
  7. What do I want more of?
  8. What is one thing to finish?
  9. What is one thing to drop?
  10. What will I protect this month?

Knowing Yourself

  1. What do I do when no one asks?
  2. What am I quietly good at?
  3. What did I believe at twenty?
  4. What changed my mind?
  5. What do I defend that is not mine?
  6. What would I do with a free year?
  7. What am I still deciding to be?
  8. What do people come to me for?
  9. What drains me every time?
  10. What is worth being slow about?

When You Feel Behind

  1. Behind whom, exactly?
  2. Whose timeline is that?
  3. What did I actually do this year?
  4. What did no one see?
  5. What was slower for a good reason?
  6. What am I comparing to a highlight?
  7. What would enough look like?
  8. What can I stop counting?
  9. What is mine to finish?
  10. What can wait a year?
