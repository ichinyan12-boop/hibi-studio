30 Journal Prompts for People Who Say Yes Too Easily

for people who say yes too easily

All 30 questions are here, free to read and use. If you would rather write them on paper, the printable version is below.

People Pleasing

  1. What did I agree to this week?
  2. Which yes was really a no?
  3. What was I afraid would happen?
  4. Whose comfort was I protecting?
  5. What did it cost me?
  6. Where do I feel it in my day?
  7. What is one thing I can hand back?
  8. What words would make that easier?
  9. Who is safe to practice with?
  10. What will I say next time?

Setting A Boundary

  1. Where do I feel the most stretched?
  2. What exactly is the limit?
  3. Whose expectation is this?
  4. What have I been saying yes to?
  5. What would I say if it were easy?
  6. What words can I borrow?
  7. When will I say it?
  8. What will I do if it is pushed?
  9. What am I afraid will happen?
  10. What is likely to happen instead?

When Work Takes Over

  1. When did today stop being mine?
  2. What did work take that I miss?
  3. What did I say yes to?
  4. What would happen if I stopped at six?
  5. Who set that expectation?
  6. What is genuinely urgent?
  7. What only feels urgent?
  8. What is one hour I can take back?
  9. What will I do with it?
  10. How will I protect it?
