30 Daily Journal Prompts for Morning and Night

a gentle rhythm for morning, night and Sunday

All 30 questions are here, free to read and use. If you would rather write them on paper, the printable version is below.

Morning

  1. What is the first thing I am carrying?
  2. Does it need me today?
  3. What is actually on today?
  4. What did I decide yesterday?
  5. What will I not re-decide?
  6. What is the one thing that matters?
  7. What can be smaller than planned?
  8. Where will I leave a gap?
  9. What do I want to notice today?
  10. How do I want today to end?

Evening

  1. What is still open from today?
  2. What can I close right now?
  3. What am I carrying that can wait?
  4. Who am I still replying to in my head?
  5. What went better than I expected?
  6. What did I not give myself credit for?
  7. What will tomorrow ask of me?
  8. What is the first small thing?
  9. What can stay undecided tonight?
  10. What do I want to leave open?

Weekly Reset

  1. What filled this week?
  2. What of it was actually mine?
  3. What did I say yes to too fast?
  4. What kept returning?
  5. What feeds that pattern?
  6. What did I protect well?
  7. Where did I not leave room?
  8. What deserves less next week?
  9. What deserves more?
  10. What one boundary would help?
