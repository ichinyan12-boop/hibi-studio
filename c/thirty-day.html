30 Day Journal Challenge

one question a day, in three quiet weeks

Here are all 30 prompts, free to read and use.

Take a few minutes with them, or save this page and come back when you need a place to begin. There is no right order and nothing you need to finish.

Days 1 To 10 Noticing

  1. What is taking up the most room in my head?
  2. What did I do today without thinking?
  3. Which part of the day felt longest?
  4. What made me tense today?
  5. What did I look forward to?
  6. Who did I think about most?
  7. What did I put off again?
  8. Where did I feel most like myself?
  9. What did I want to say and did not?
  10. What pattern am I starting to see?

Days 11 To 20 Honesty

  1. What am I tired of pretending?
  2. What do I want that I have not admitted?
  3. Which relationship needs a real conversation?
  4. What am I doing only out of habit?
  5. What am I afraid people would think?
  6. What do I envy, and what does it tell me?
  7. What have I outgrown?
  8. What do I keep proving, and to whom?
  9. What would I change if no one minded?
  10. What is true even when it is inconvenient?

Days 21 To 30 Change

  1. What is one thing I will stop?
  2. What is one thing I will start?
  3. What boundary do I need this month?
  4. How will I say it, in one sentence?
  5. What support do I need to ask for?
  6. What will I protect in my week?
  7. What does a good ordinary day look like?
  8. What is the smallest first step?
  9. How will I know it is working?
  10. What do I want to remember from these 30 days?
