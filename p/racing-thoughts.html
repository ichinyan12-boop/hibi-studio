10 journal prompts for racing thoughts

a brain dump, then a little order

When your thoughts will not slow down at night, do a brain dump first, then bring a little order to it.

  1. What is loudest right now?
  2. Write it all down. Nothing is too small.
  3. Which one belongs to someone else?
  4. What only needs a place, not a decision?
  5. What am I afraid of if I stop?
  6. What did I decide today, but keep re-deciding?
  7. What is unfinished, and does it matter?
  8. What would I tell a friend saying this?
  9. What can wait until morning?
  10. What will I leave open tomorrow?
