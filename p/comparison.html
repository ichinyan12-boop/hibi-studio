For the scroll that leaves a mark.

  1. Who was I looking at?
  2. What exactly did I want?
  3. Is it the thing, or the ease?
  4. What did I not see behind it?
  5. What do I already have of it?
  6. What would I trade for it?
  7. What am I unwilling to trade?
  8. What is one step toward it?
  9. What can I stop looking at?
  10. What is mine that no one sees?
