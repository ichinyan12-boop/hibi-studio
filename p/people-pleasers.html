for the yes you did not mean

For the yes you did not mean, and the words that make handing it back easier.

  1. What did I agree to this week?
  2. Which yes was really a no?
  3. What was I afraid would happen?
  4. Whose comfort was I protecting?
  5. What did it cost me?
  6. Where do I feel it in my day?
  7. What is one thing I can hand back?
  8. What words would make that easier?
  9. Who is safe to practice with?
  10. What will I say next time?
