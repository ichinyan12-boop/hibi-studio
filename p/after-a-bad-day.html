10 journal prompts after a bad day

when you do not want to talk about it

For the evening after a day that did not go well.

  1. What actually happened?
  2. What did I make it mean?
  3. Which part was mine?
  4. Which part was never mine?
  5. What did I need and not get?
  6. What did I need and not ask for?
  7. What would I say to a friend?
  8. What is still true, even today?
  9. What can I put down before bed?
  10. What is one kind thing I can do now?
