Mono no aware

もののあはれ · mo-no no a-wa-re · a tender awareness of things passing and changing

Mono no aware is a complex literary and aesthetic idea associated with a sensitive awareness of the transience of things. It can involve a tender or bittersweet response to change, loss, beauty, and the passing of time, rather than simply meaning sadness.

It offers a way to write about moments that matter partly because they will not last. The subject can be as small as a season, a room, or a familiar person changing over time.

Prompts to write against it

  1. What am I noticing because it will change?
  2. What ordinary moment feels precious now?
  3. What has already passed that I still carry?
  4. What am I learning to let change?
  5. What do I want to remember before it is different?
